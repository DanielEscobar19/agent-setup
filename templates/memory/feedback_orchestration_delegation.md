Delegate implementation to the junior and mid agents; reserve the senior agent for design and review.

**Why:** Cost and speed; senior-tier reasoning is only worth it for ambiguous or correctness-critical work.
**How to apply:** When a task is scoped, dispatch to junior/mid; bring in senior for architecture decisions and reviewing the result.
